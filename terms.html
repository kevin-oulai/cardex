Independent fan project

CARDEX is a free, unofficial game about real-world cars. It is not sponsored, endorsed, licensed, or approved by any automotive brand or tuner. Brand and model names are used to identify the vehicles depicted.

Optional online accounts and rankings

CARDEX is introducing optional Supabase-based accounts. Online display names and verified leaderboard results may be public. Existing local saves and Showdown scores are not automatically eligible for online ranking or global serial numbers. Server-issued card serials and verified rankings will become available only after the required backend services are deployed. Users must not impersonate others or attempt to manipulate rankings, accounts, or serial issuance.

Cloud collections and online accounts

CARDEX now requires an online account for gameplay and stores game progress in account-bound cloud storage. Old local saves are not automatically transferred. Cloud-synced game state is not currently verified competitive ownership; global serial issuance and authenticated leaderboard submissions remain under development. The publisher should provide a documented account-deletion and data-access process.

Game and virtual items

CARDEX currently offers no purchases with real money. Booster tickets, credits, cards, rarity, and progress are virtual game mechanics without any promised cash value, transferability, or redemption. Game rules and availability may change during development.

Images and attribution

Some vehicle photographs are obtained from Wikimedia projects, and others may be configured through the game's image catalog. Use the IMAGE SOURCE link shown with an image to inspect its original source. Photo rights and license conditions vary by image. An image appearing in the game does not mean CARDEX owns it or that the image may be freely reused. Image attribution and license verification remain a work in progress; report incorrect or disputed images through the repository issues.

Code licensing

Making the repository publicly viewable does not by itself grant an open-source license. Consult the repository's current LICENSE file, if present, for the applicable terms. Third-party images and trademarks are not automatically covered by any code license.

Availability and accuracy

CARDEX is provided as an experimental game. Vehicle specifications and images may contain errors. Back up your save regularly; browser-local progress is not guaranteed to persist.
