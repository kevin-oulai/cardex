Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Required online accounts (Supabase)

If you create an online account, CARDEX uses Supabase (Supabase, Inc.) for authentication and database services. Supabase processes your email address, password authentication credentials (handled by Supabase Auth), account identifier, and session information. The online database may store your public display name, server-issued card serials, and verified leaderboard scores as part of online gameplay. Your email is not displayed in the public leaderboard. Public display names, verified scores, UTC first-clear times, the car used, model-scoped serial numbers, account creation month, collection counts, a selection of high-rarity cards and recent verified Showdown results may be visible to signed-in players through the leaderboard and public collector profiles.

Online accounts are required for gameplay. Older local saves are not automatically converted into verified online cards. Supabase may process IP addresses, authentication logs, and service metadata for security and operation. See the Supabase privacy policy. The publisher must still document applicable legal bases, data-retention periods, international transfers, account deletion and data-subject contact arrangements before treating the online service as fully compliant.

Account-bound cloud saves

CARDEX now requires an account to play. Game progress, collection, virtual balances, profile preferences, wishlist and Showdown progress are stored in a Supabase account-bound database record. A browser cache may be retained for recovery if synchronization fails. The earlier manual export/import controls have been retired. Existing local-only saves are not automatically imported. Cloud game state is currently client-generated and is not treated as proof of verified competitive ownership or scores.

Account deletion and data-retention procedures still require publisher review and implementation. Do not assume deleting local browser data removes cloud records.

Local browser data

The browser may retain account-scoped cached progress and session data. Clearing site data signs you out or clears the local cache but does not delete your Supabase account or server-issued collection.

Data transmitted to third parties

The site is delivered by GitHub Pages, which may process connection information such as IP addresses and access logs under its own policies. CARDEX currently requests some vehicle photographs and metadata from Wikipedia/Wikimedia services, which may receive your IP address, browser information, and request metadata. Review GitHub's privacy statement and the Wikimedia privacy policy.

Advertising and tracking

CARDEX does not currently integrate advertising networks or advertising cookies. If advertising or non-essential tracking is introduced, this policy must be updated and any legally required consent controls must be implemented before those technologies are activated. This page is not an advertising-consent mechanism.

Retention and controls

Local game data stays in your browser until you clear it, replace your save, or the browser removes it. CARDEX provides Supabase-backed player accounts but does not yet provide an in-game server-side account deletion service. You can manage site data through your browser settings.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, use the publisher's contact details on the legal notice once provided. This policy describes the present static-site implementation and should be reviewed when new features or external services are added.
