Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Required online accounts (Supabase)

If you create an online account, CARDEX uses Supabase (Supabase, Inc.) for authentication and database services. Supabase processes your email address, password authentication credentials (handled by Supabase Auth), account identifier, and session information. The online database may store your public display name, server-issued card serials, and verified leaderboard scores as part of online gameplay. Your email is not displayed in the public leaderboard. Public display names, verified scores, UTC first-clear times, the car used, model-scoped serial numbers, account creation month, collection counts, a selection of high-rarity cards and recent verified Showdown results may be visible to signed-in players through the leaderboard and public collector profiles. You can hide your collection counts, featured cards and recent Showdown history using the privacy toggle in Profile. Leaderboard name, score, challenger and defeat time remain public.

Online accounts are required for gameplay. Older local saves are not automatically converted into verified online cards. Supabase may process IP addresses, authentication logs, and service metadata for security and operation. See the Supabase privacy policy. The publisher must still confirm and publish applicable legal bases, retention schedules or criteria, transfer safeguards where relevant, and a private contact channel for data rights requests. These disclosures are not yet complete.

Collector titles and visibility

Collector titles are calculated from current card inventory, claimed achievements and recorded daily Showdown standings. An equipped title may appear on your public collector profile when collection visibility is enabled. Private title progression is not available to other accounts through the title service. Showdown leaderboard results remain visible as described above.

Account-bound cloud saves

CARDEX now requires an account to play. Server-issued cards, wallet balances, verified Showdown results, achievement claims and equipped titles are recorded by Supabase. Profile preferences, wishlist, showroom choices and other supported state use versioned account-bound cloud saves. An account-scoped browser cache may be retained for recovery. When a newer save exists on another device, the app can reload the server version and preserve a local backup of conflicting edits; automatic merging is not implemented. The earlier manual export/import controls have been retired. Existing local-only saves are not automatically imported. Some preferences originate in the browser, but card ownership, virtual balances, achievement claims, title eligibility and competitive results are verified by server-side operations.

You can permanently delete your account from the Account page after re-entering your password and confirming the request. This removes your Supabase account and associated game data, including your collection and leaderboard history. Supabase or hosting-provider security logs and backups may remain subject to their separate retention schedules. Do not assume deleting local browser data removes cloud records.

Local browser data

The browser may retain account-scoped cached progress and session data. Clearing site data signs you out or clears the local cache but does not delete your Supabase account or server-issued collection.

Data transmitted to third parties

The site is delivered by GitHub Pages, which may process connection information such as IP addresses and access logs under its own policies. CARDEX currently requests some vehicle photographs and metadata from Wikipedia/Wikimedia services, which may receive your IP address, browser information, and request metadata. Review GitHub's privacy statement and the Wikimedia privacy policy.

Advertising and tracking

CARDEX does not currently integrate advertising networks or advertising cookies. If advertising or non-essential tracking is introduced, this policy must be updated and any legally required consent controls must be implemented before those technologies are activated. This page is not an advertising-consent mechanism.

Retention and controls

Local cached preferences and conflict backups remain in your browser until site data is cleared or the browser removes them; they may persist after signing out. CARDEX provides an in-game account deletion control on the Account page. Account deletion removes active database records linked to your account. You can also manage local site data through your browser settings. Data retained in third-party logs or backups is subject to those providers' policies.

Contact and updates

The publisher has not yet provided a dedicated private privacy contact channel. For non-confidential issues, see the legal notice; do not post personal data in public GitHub Issues. A private rights-request channel is still required. This policy describes the present static-site implementation and should be reviewed when new features or external services are added.
